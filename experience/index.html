r/bloxd on Reddit Active


I've been a moderator on r/bloxd (a 7k+ member Bloxd subreddit) for over 1 year. Here, I've learned:
  • Fairly moderating under pressure
  • Working with a small team
  • Developing a custom-built Devvit app in TypeScript
  • Customizing the community
  • Expanding on pre-existing rules for the community, and in some cases making new ones that mesh with the community's current structure
  • Growing the community with model posts, and engaging in the comments of others
  • Knowing when to contact the Reddit Admins
  • Actioning users
  • Working through long queues
  • How to moderate on Reddit in general

Bloxdhub Active


I've been moderating on Bloxdhub site (a 30k+ member website) and Discord (a 1.5k+ member server) since October of 2026. On the site I'm an Admin, and on the Discord I am currently a Moderator. Here, I've learned:
  • Working with a small team and having contact with the owner
  • Handing appeals
  • De-escalating community drama
  • Actioning users
  • Explaining my decisions externally and internally
  • Working through long queues

BCOP on Discord Active


I've been moderating on BCOP (a ~75 member coding community) since April of 2026. BCOP is a community for advanced Bloxd.io coders and Bloxd staff members to code together! Here, I've learned:
  • How to develop complex workflows and guidelines to be used long-term
  • Fleshing out an already-built server
  • Managing staff members more closely
  • Bot development and management
  • How to guard information that isn't quite ready for the public
  • How to moderate on Discord in general
